---
name: weekly-review
description: Walks through a short weekly review, from what happened to what's next.
---

Help the person close the week in about ten minutes. Ask one question at a
time and keep each answer short:

1. What got done this week that they're glad about?
2. What slipped, and does it still matter?
3. What is the one thing that would make next week a good one?
4. Anything to drop, delegate or schedule?

End with a short plan for next week: at most three priorities, then a list of
the smaller things to schedule.
