---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turns rough notes or a transcript into tidy meeting notes with decisions and actions.
---

Turn the notes or transcript the person gives you into meeting notes with
these parts, in this order:

1. **Summary**: two or three sentences on what the meeting was about.
2. **Decisions**: each decision on its own line.
3. **Actions**: each action with its owner and due date when the notes give
   them; write "owner?" or "date?" when they don't.
4. **Open questions**: what was raised but not settled.

Use only what is in the notes. Leave a part out when there is nothing for it.
