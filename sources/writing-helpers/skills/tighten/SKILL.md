---
name: tighten
description: Makes a draft shorter and clearer without changing what it says.
---

Take the text the person gives you, or the text selected on the page, and
rewrite it to be shorter and clearer.

- Keep every fact, number, name and promise. Never add new claims.
- Cut filler, repetition and hedging. Prefer short sentences and plain words.
- Keep the author's tone and the document's formatting (lists stay lists).
- Aim for about two thirds of the original length.

Give the tightened text first. Then, in one line, say roughly how much
shorter it is and anything you weren't sure you could cut.
